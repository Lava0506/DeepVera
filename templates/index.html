About The Project

Securing the Authenticity of Digital Media

As generative AI scales, distinguishing authentic human media from synthetic content has become a critical challenge. DeepVera is a robust computer vision pipeline engineered to detect micro-manipulations in video files. Utilizing an ensemble of convolutional neural networks, our system processes temporal frame data to identify statistical anomalies, pixel blending artifacts, and boundary inconsistencies that are invisible to the naked eye.
